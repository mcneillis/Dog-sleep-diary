What Settle is

Settle helps you keep nightly notes and optional clips for your own vet visit. It is not a diagnosis, not a vet service, and not a medical device.

  1. Nightly notes — bedtime, wakes, what you saw. Unscored. Your words, not ours.
  2. Optional video with sound — you choose what to add to your Hand-in pack.
  3. Hand-in pack — notes and clips you prepare to take to your own vet. This is not a send, and not a clinical report.
